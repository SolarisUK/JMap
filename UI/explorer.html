Read the balance. A surplus means annual generation exceeded gross demand. A deficit means local generation was lower. Gross demand includes losses and the electricity used by generation and storage.

Read the coverage. 150% means annual generation equals 1.5 times annual demand. It does not mean every hour is covered or that households have better access.

[ Understanding vulnerability ]

Does a deficit mean a shortage?

A nation can meet demand through transfers and imports. An annual deficit signals reliance on supply from elsewhere; it is not evidence of unmet demand.

Local shortage riskNot assessed

Assessing actual shortage risk needs peak demand, available generation, storage duration, interconnector availability and network constraints.
