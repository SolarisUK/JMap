Sources and map method

Each site comes from an official operating-asset register. Capacity describes installed equipment, not current output or guaranteed supply. Small installations and subsequent changes may be absent.

Overview adds the published capacities within each source region. Its markers represent regional summaries, not power stations or exact service boundaries. Offshore sites are a separate UK-wide group; their summary marker is not a single location. Filtered totals use the same fixed size scale, with summary positions held constant for comparison.

Sites shows the underlying locations. All bubbles represent installed capacity; nearby sites may share a capacity-total bubble until you zoom closer. No map number represents a site count. The side panel supplies names, capacities, record counts and technology breakdowns. Reset map returns to Overview without clearing your other filters.

The GB snapshot requests public Elexon FUELINST observations directly. It uses one interval and identifies missing or stale readings. National generation is never allocated to individual sites.

The simulation is separate and does not generate this map’s data.
