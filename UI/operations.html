    Data and interpretation

    Map colours measure unmet demand, unserved_mw. A zone can import electricity and still meet all demand. Signed arrows show corridor flow relative to its declared from/to direction. Overloaded lines exceed 100%; tripped lines have a distinct dashed treatment, and a zero-limit line has no flow arrows.

    The four top metrics are cumulative episode totals at the selected half-hour. The Results tab compares completed 48-step episodes. All timestamps are stored in UTC and displayed in Europe/London.

    The local-authority statistics remain available in Energy context; those historical annual figures do not drive this simulation.

    Geography: Natural Earth, public domain. Network geometry and model assumptions come from the supplied project contract.
