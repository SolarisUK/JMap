Live data and map method

Moving arrows show the recorded direction. Animation speed does not represent MW or electricity travel time.

This page requests the signed Elexon FUELINST dataset directly from your browser. It reports operational metering supplied by NESO, averaged over five minutes. Checks run every 60 seconds; this is not second-by-second telemetry.

Every route and total uses one observation interval. A link missing from that interval is unknown; an older value is never inserted into a newer total. Partial totals cover only reporting links. If updates fail, a previous observation stays clearly labelled with its time. There is no generated or simulated fallback.

Positive MW means power entering Great Britain; negative MW means power leaving it. Moyle connects GB to Northern Ireland, which is part of the UK and operates in the all-island electricity market. Zero measured flow does not establish whether a link is available.

Lines join approximate terminal areas, positioned for a readable schematic. They are not surveyed cable routes or exact station coordinates. The selected-link reference identifies the actual terminal locations. No internal GB transfers or local power shortages are inferred from cross-border flow.
